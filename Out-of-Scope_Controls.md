## List of Out-of-Scope Controls

Controls of COBIT 2019 and ITIL 4 that were considered but deliberately not mapped in the Agile Control Map, with the reasoning for each exclusion. Controls are referenced by identifier and title only; please consult the original publications for their full text.

| Framework | Control ID | Title | Reasoning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| COBIT 2019 | APO01.01 | Design the management system for enterprise I&T. | The management system for enterprise I&T has to be established outside an IT project implementing an IT solution. It has to be established before starting any initiatives. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO01.02 | Communicate management objectives, direction and decisions made. | IT initiatives are on the receiving end of this communication on management objectives, directions and decisions made. This communication is not a task that can be or should be planned in any way by an IT initiative. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO01.03 | Implement management processes (to support the achievement of governance and management objectives). | The implementation of management processes, which should support the achievement of governance and management objectives, is not a task that can be anticipated or planned by an IT initiative. For this reason it is out-of-scope. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO01.04 | Define and implement the organizational structures. | The implementation of organizational structures (e.g., committees) according to the management system design, is out-of-scope to an artifact supporting IT initiatives, since these committees have to be established upfront to IT projects, to set the direction. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO01.05 | Establish roles and responsibilities. | Even though putting this management practice out-of-scope is debatable, the mentioned roles that should be established here are not temporary roles, like in a project, but functional ones. But also if temporary project roles and responsibilities would be included here as well, the establishment and definition of those should not be subject of an IT initiative itself. It is the other way round. The IT initiative/project should be subject of a governance system that defines the roles within the IT initiative. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO01.06 | Optimize the placement of the IT function. | The placement of the IT function should not be subject of an IT initiative or project implementing an IT solution. The IT function should be in charge of governing IT initiatives. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO01.09 | Define and communicate policies and procedures. | The definition and communication of policies and procedures is not subject of an IT initiative/IT project. The IT project has to follow respective policies and procedures, but shouldn't define or establish them. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO01.10 | Define and implement infrastructure, services and applications to support the governance and management system. | This management practice is out-of-scope for IT-initiatives, since it might be in scope for an IT project implementing exactly this management system or management structure, but the general IT project is subject of a management system and should not implement these things out of a conflict of interest. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO01.11 | Manage continual improvement of the I&T management system. | Also in this case, the general IT initiative should be subject of a continual improvement of the I&T management system, but should just be able to propose improvements of this system and not implement them, due to an inherent conflict of interest. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO03.05 | Provide enterprise architecture services. | The provision of enterprise architecture services, including guidance and monitoring of projects in a formalized way, cannot be part of the agile solution development project itself. It has to be provided by the regular processes and the project just has to contribute to it. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO04.01 | Create an environment conducive to innovation. | Creating an innovation friendly environment within an enterprise cannot be in scope of an agile solution development project, particularly defining culture, reward, collaboration, technology forums, and promotion mechanisms. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO04.02 | Maintain an understanding of the enterprise environment. | Of course, agile solution development projects have to inherently work with relevant stakeholders, but within the context of the project, not on a blank page, as is here the intention to enable opportunities by new technologies. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO04.03 | Monitor and scan the technology environment. | The monitoring of new technologies in a systematic manner cannot be performed by an agile solution development project. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO04.04 | Assess the potential of emerging technologies and innovative ideas. | Analyzing emerging technologies and develop new innovative suggestions cannot be part of an agile solution development project, otherwise it would likely result in scope creep. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO04.05 | Recommend appropriate further initiatives. | It is not up to an agile solution development project to recommend further initiatives based on technological innovation. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO04.06 | Monitor the implementation and use of innovation. | It cannot be in scope of an agile solution development project to monitor and use innovations. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO05.01 | Determine the availability and sources of funds. | The determination of funding options on an enterprise level, cannot be in scope of an agile solution development project. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO05.02 | Evaluate and select programs to fund. | The selection of programs to be funded on an enterprise level, cannot be in scope of an agile solution development project. Even more so, the project would have a conflict of interest. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO05.03 | Monitor, optimize and report on investment portfolio performance. | The evaluation of the investment portfolio is an important task but cannot be in scope of an agile solution development project. It has to be done from outside the project. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO05.04 | Maintain portfolios. | Maintaining the portfolio of investment programs and projects cannot be in scope of the agile solution development project, even though the project has to report and contribute to the function collecting it, but on initiative of the function. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO05.05 | Manage benefits achievement. | Monitoring the benefits achievement cannot be in scope of an agile solution development project, since centralized functions have to collect and compare all the initiatives centrally. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO06.01 | Manage finance and accounting. | The establishment of a method for I&T-related costs, investments and depreciation as part of enterprise financial systems and accounts, cannot be in scope of an agile solution development project. The project has to use these mechanisms, but the establishment should not be of their concern. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO07.01 | Acquire and maintain adequate and appropriate staffing. | Tasks like the general methodological setup for resources management, recruitment processes, retention processes, flexible resource arrangements and background checks, have to be established by the HR function for the entire company. The project itself can't and shouldn't take over this task, also for a principle of equality. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO11.01 | Establish a quality management system (QMS). | The establishment of an enterprise quality management system cannot be part of an agile solution development project. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO11.05 | Maintain continuous improvement. | The maintenance and regular communication of an overall quality plan cannot be in scope of an agile solution development project. Even if it is run in a DevOps configuration, there should be other company wide mechanisms addressing control. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO13.01 | Establish and maintain an information security management system (ISMS). | The establishment of an entire ISMS cannot be in scope of general agile solution development projects. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO14.01 | Define and communicate the organization’s data management strategy and roles and responsibilities. | The definition and communication of a data management strategy is not the responsibility of an agile solution development project. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO14.02 | Define and maintain a consistent business glossary. | Agile solution development projects have to align and potentially update the business glossary. | COBIT 2019 | APO14.05 | Establish data profiling methodologies, processes and tools. | The establishment of standardized data profiling methodologies, processes, practices, tools and templates is not in scope of a generic agile solution development project. |

| COBIT 2019 | BAI10.01 | Establish and maintain a configuration model. | The establishment of a logical configuration model cannot be in scope of a generic agile solution development project. It has to be established before the project can comply with configuration management requirements. |
