## List of Out-of-Scope Controls

| Framework | Control ID | Title | Management Practice | Reasoning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| COBIT 2019 | APO01.01 | Design the management system for enterprise I&T. | Design a management system tailored to the needs of the enterprise. Management needs of the enterprise are defined through the use of the goals cascade and by application of design factors. Ensure the governance components are integrated and aligned with the enterprise’s governance and management philosophy and operating style. | The management system for enterprise I&T has to be established outside an IT project implementing an IT solution. It has to be established before starting any initiatives. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO01.02 | Communicate management objectives, direction and decisions made. | Communicate awareness and promote understanding of alignment and I&T objectives to stakeholders throughout the enterprise. Communicate at regular intervals on important I&T-related decisions and their impact for the organization. | IT initiatives are on the receiving end of this communication on management objectives, directions and descisions made. This commuication is not a task that can be or should be planned in any way by an IT initiative. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO01.03 | Implement management processes (to support the achievement of governance and management objectives). | Define target process capability levels and implementation priority based on the management system design. | The implementation of management processes, which should support the achievement of governance and management objectives, is not a task that can be anticipated or planned by an IT initiative. For this reason it is out-of-scope. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO01.04 | Define and implement the organizational structures. | Put in place the required internal and extended organizational structures (e.g., committees) per the management system design, enabling effective and efficient decision making. Ensure that required technology and information knowledge is included in the composition of management structures. | The implementation of organizational structures (e.g., committees) according to the management system design, is out-of-scope to an artifact supporting IT inititives, since these committees have to be established upfront to IT projects, to set the direction. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO01.05 | Establish roles and responsibilities. | Define and communicate roles and responsibilities for enterprise I&T, including authority levels, responsibilities and accountability. | Eventhough putting this management practice out-of-scope is debateable, the mentioned roles that should be established here are not temporary roles, like in a project, put functional ones. But also if temporary project roles and responsiblities would be included here as well, the establishment and definition of those should not be subject of an IT iniative itself. It is the other way round. The IT iniative/project should be subject of a governance system that defines the roles within the IT initiative. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO01.06 | Optimize the placement of the IT function. | Position the IT capabilities in the overall organizational structure to reflect the strategic importance and operational dependency of IT within the enterprise. The reporting line of the CIO and representation of IT within senior management should be commensurate with the importance of I&T within the enterprise. | The placement of the IT function should not be subject of an IT initiative or project implementing an IT solution. The IT function should be in charge of governing IT initiatives. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO01.09 | Define and communicate policies and procedures. | Put in place procedures to maintain compliance with and performance measurement of policies and other components of the control framework. Enforce the consequences of noncompliance or inadequate performance. Track trends and performance and consider these in the future design and improvement of the control framework. | The definition and communication of policies and procedures is not subject of an IT initiative/IT project. The IT project has to follow respective policies and procedures, but shouldn't define or establish them. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO01.10 | Define and implement infrastructure, services and applications to support the governance and management system. | Define and implement infrastructure, services and applications to support the governance and management system (e.g., architecture repositories, risk management system, project management tools, costtracking tools and incident monitoring tools). | This management practice is out-of-scope for IT-initiatives, since it might be in scope for an IT project implementing exactly this management system or management structure, but the general IT project is subject of a management system and should not implement this things out of a conflict of interest. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO01.11 | Manage continual improvement of the I&T management system. | Continually improve processes and other management system components to ensure that they can deliver against governance and management objectives. Consider COBIT implementation guidance, emerging standards, compliance requirements, automation opportunities and the feedback of stakeholders. | Also in this case, the general IT initiative should be subject of a continual improvement of the I&T management system, but should just be able to propose improvements of this system and not implement them, due to an inherent conflict of interest. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO03.05 | Provide enterprise architecture services. | Provide enterprise architecture services within the enterprise that include guidance to and monitoring of implementation projects, formalizing ways of working through architecture contracts, and measuring and communicating architecture’s value and compliance monitoring. | The provision of enterprise architecture services, including guidance and monitoring of projects in a fomalized way, cannot be part of the agile solution delvelopment project itself. It has to be provided by the regular processes and the projects has just to contribute to it. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO04.01 | Create an environment conducive to innovation. | Create an environment that is conducive to innovation, considering methods such as culture, reward, collaboration, technology forums, and mechanisms to promote and capture employee ideas. | Creating an innovation friendly environment within an enterprise cannot be in scope of an agile solution development project, particular defining culture, reward, collaboration, technology forums, and promition mechanisms. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO04.02 | Maintain an understanding of the enterprise environment. | Work with relevant stakeholders to understand their challenges. Maintain an adequate understanding of enterprise strategy, competitive environment and other constraints, so that opportunities enabled by new technologies can be identified. | Of course agile solution development projects have to inherently work with relevant stakeholders, but within the context of the project, not on a blank page, as is here the intention to enable opportunities by new technologies. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO04.03 | Monitor and scan the technology environment. | Set up a technology watch process to perform systematic monitoring and scanning of the enterprise’s external environment to identify emerging technologies that have the potential to create value (e.g., by realizing the enterprise strategy, optimizing costs, avoiding obsolescence, and better enabling enterprise and I&T processes). Monitor the marketplace, competitive landscape, industry sectors, and legal and regulatory trends to be able to analyze emerging technologies or innovation ideas in the enterprise context. | The monitoring of new technolgoies in a systematic manner cannot be performed by an agile solution development project. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO04.04 | Assess the potential of emerging technologies and innovative ideas. | Analyze identified emerging technologies and/or other I&T innovative suggestions to understand their business potential. Work with stakeholders to validate assumptions on the potential of new technologies and innovation. | Analyzing emerging technologies and develop new innovative suggestions cannot be part of an agile solution development project, otherwise it would likely result in scope creep. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO04.05 | Recommend appropriate further initiatives. | Evaluate and monitor the results of proof-of-concept initiatives and, if favorable, generate recommendations for further initiatives. Gain stakeholder support. | It is not up to an agile solution delvelopment project to recommend further initiatives based on technological innovation. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO04.06 | Monitor the implementation and use of innovation. | Monitor the implementation and use of emerging technologies and innovations during adoption, integration and for the full economic life cycle to ensure that the promised benefits are realized and to identify lessons learned. | It cannot be in scope of an agile solution development project to monitor and use innovations. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO05.01 | Determine the availability and sources of funds. | Determine potential sources of funds, different funding options and the implications of the funding source on the investment return expectations. | The determination of funding options on an enterprise level, cannot be in scope of an agile solution development project. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO05.02 | Evaluate and select programs to fund. | Based on requirements for the overall investment portfolio mix and the I&T strategic plan and road map, evaluate and prioritize program business cases and decide on investment proposals. Allocate funds and initiate programs. | The selection of programs to be funded on an enterprise level, cannot be in scope of an agile solution development project. Even more so, the project would have a conflict of interest. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO05.03 | Monitor, optimize and report on investment portfolio performance. | On a regular basis, monitor and optimize the performance of the investment portfolio and individual programs throughout the entire investment life cycle. Ensure continuous follow-up on the alignment of the portfolio with I&T strategy. | The evaluation of the investment portfolio is an important task but cannot be in scope of an agile solution developement project. It has to be done from outside the project. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO05.04 | Maintain portfolios. | Maintain portfolios of investment programs and projects, I&T products and services, and I&T assets. | Maintaining the portfolio of investment programs and projects cannot be in scope of the agile solution development project, eventhough the project has to report and contribute to the function collecting it, but on initiative of the function. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO05.05 | Manage benefits achievement. | Monitor the benefits of providing and maintaining appropriate I&T products, services and capabilities, based on the agreed and current business case. | Monitoring the benefits achievement cannot be in scope of an agile solution development project, since a centralized functions have to collect and compare all the initiatives centrally. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO06.01 | Manage finance and accounting. | Establish and maintain a method to manage and account for all I&T-related costs, investments and depreciation as an integral part of enterprise financial systems and accounts. Report using the enterprise’s financial measurement systems. | The establishment of a method for I&T-related costs, investments and depreciation as part of entrise financial systems and accounts, cannot be in scope of an agile solution development project. The project has to use these mechiansisms, but the establishment should not be of their concern. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO07.01 | Acquire and maintain adequate and appropriate staffing. | Establish and maintain a method to manage and account for all I&Trelated costs, investments and depreciation as an integral part of the enterprise financial systems and accounts. Report using the enterprise’s financial measurement systems. | Tasks like the general methodological setup for resources management, recruitment processes, retention processes, flexible resource arrangements and background checks, have to be established by the HR function for the entire company. The project itself can't and shouldn't take over this task, also for a principle of equality. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO11.01 | Establish a quality management system (QMS). | Establish and maintain a quality management system (QMS) that provides a standard, formal and continuous approach to quality management of information. The QMS should enable technology and business processes to align with business requirements and enterprise quality management. | The establishment of an enterprise quality management system cannot be part of an agile solution development project. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO11.05 | Maintain continuous improvement. | Maintain and regularly communicate an overall quality plan that promotes continuous improvement. The plan should define the need for, and benefits of, continuous improvement. Collect and analyze data about the quality management system (QMS) and improve its effectiveness. Correct nonconformities to prevent recurrence. | The maintainance and regularly communication of an overal quality plan cannot be in scope of an agile solution development project. Even if it is run in a DevOps configuration, there should be other company wide mechianisms adressing control. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO13.01 | Establish and maintain an information security management system (ISMS). | Establish and maintain an information security management system (ISMS) that provides a standard, formal and continuous approach to information security management, enabling secure technology and business processes that are aligned with business requirements. | The establishment of an entire ISMS cannot be in scope of general agile solution development projects. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO14.01 | Define and communicate the organization’s data management strategy and roles and responsibilities. | Define how to manage and improve the organization’s data assets, in line with enterprise strategy and objectives. Communicate the data management strategy to all stakeholders. Assign roles and responsibilities to ensure that corporate data are managed as critical assets and the data management strategy is implemented and maintained in an effective and sustainable manner. | The definition and communication of a data management strategy is not the responsibility of an agile solution development project. | 
| COBIT 2019 | APO14.02 | Define and maintain a consistent business glossary. | Create, approve, update and promote consistent business terms and definitions to foster shared data usage across the organization. | Agile solution development projects have to align and potentially update the business glossary. However the process of creating, approving, updating and promoting consistent business terms cannot be in scope of an agile solution development project. | 
| COBIT 2019 | APO14.03 | Establish the processes and infrastructure for metadata management. | Establish the processes and infrastructure for specifying and extending metadata about the organization’s data assets, fostering and supporting data sharing, ensuring compliant use of data, improving responsiveness to business changes and reducing data-related risk. | The establishment of processes and infrastructure for metadata management cannot be in scope of a generic agile solution development project. | 
| COBIT 2019 | APO14.04 | Define a data quality strategy. | Define an integrated, organizationwide strategy to achieve and maintain the level of data quality (such as complexity, integrity, accuracy, completeness, validity, traceability and timeliness) required to support the business goals and objectives. | The definition of an integrated, organizationwide data strategy cannot be in scope of an agile solution development project. | 
| COBIT 2019 | APO14.05 | Establish data profiling methodologies, processes and tools. | Implement standardized data profiling methodologies, processes, practices, tools and templates that can be applied across multiple data repositories and data stores. | The establishment of standardized data profiling methodologies, processes, practices, tools and templates is not in scope off a generic agile solution development project. |
| COBIT 2019 | APO14.06 | Ensure a data quality assessment approach. | Provide a systematic approach to measure and evaluate data quality according to processes and techniques, and against data quality rules. | A systemic approach to measure and evaluate data quality has to be defined outside an agile solution development project. |
| COBIT 2019 | BAI04.01 | Assess current availability, performance and capacity and create a baseline. | Assess availability, performance and capacity of services and resources to ensure that cost-justifiable capacity and performance are available to support business needs and deliver against service level agreements (SLAs). Create availability, performance and capacity baselines for future comparison. | The assessment of the current availability, performance and capacity cannot be in scope of agile solution development projects. It has to be a regular process. |
| COBIT 2019 | BAI10.01 | Establish and maintain a configuration model. | Establish and maintain a logical model of the services, assets, infrastructure and recording of configuration items (CIs), including the relationships among them. Include the CIs considered necessary to manage services effectively and to provide a single, reliable description of the assets in a service. | The establishment of a logical configuration model cannot be in scope of a generic agile solution development project. It has to be established before the project can comply to configuration management requirements. |
| COBIT 2019 | DSS02.01 | Define classification schemes for incidents and service requests. | Define classification schemes and models for incidents and service requests. | The definition of a classification scheme and model for incidents and service requests cannot be in scope of a generic agile solution development project. |
| COBIT 2019 | DSS02.01 | Define classification schemes for incidents and service requests. | Define classification schemes for incidents and service requests. | Classification schemes for incidents and service requests have to be set up before and not in course of an generic agile solution development project. |
| COBIT 2019 | DSS02.02 | Record, classify and prioritize requests and incidents. | Identify, record and classify service requests and incidents and assign a priority according to business criticality and service agreements. | Even though this task might fall possibly in the responsiblity of an DevOps setup, the management practice is still considered out of scope of a generic agile solution development project, since the identification, recording and calssification most likely accures in the service practices according to ITIL and in usual IT processes rather than in projects. |
| COBIT 2019 | DSS02.03 | Verify, approve and fulfill service requests. | Select the appropriate request procedures and verify that the service requests fulfill defined request criteria. Obtain approval, if required, and fulfill the requests. | Service requests might result in new or adapted capabilities or features, which might be implemented in course of an agile solution development project. Perform recovery actions to restore the I&T-related service. | Incidents might result in new or adapted capabilities or features, which might be implemented in course of an agile solution development project. However, the resolution or recovery of an incident cannot be assigned to every generic epic/capability/feature. It has to be driven by a general incident management process. |

Examine trends to provide information for continual improvement. | Tracking, analyzing and reporting of incidents and service requests has to be part of general ITIL practices and cannot be performed in every generic agile solution development project. |

Include problem classification, categorization and prioritization. | Identification and classification of problems cannot be assigned to every generic epic/capability/features.
